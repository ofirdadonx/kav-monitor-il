# Changelog

The release workflow publishes the `## <version>` section below when you push a tag `v<version>`.

## 0.8

- Opening a line shows its stops, route and live buses. A watch starts only from התחל מעקב.
- Favorite lines stay at the top of the home screen.
- Blue theme. Map buses use a yellow plate and a round icon. A compact map stays on the line screen.
- Walking distance is shown in meters. Straight-line distance is not shown.
- A sent alert is one line on the watch screen; details open in a dialog.
- Shorter notification text. Skeleton placeholders while data loads.
- Home recents are lines you opened. Nearby stops stay collapsed, under recents.
- Search fields and buttons are rounded. Empty states replace plain text.
- The compact map sits under the first card. It pans in place; a corner button opens it full screen.
- The route line on the map is visible. The back button is on the right of the blue bar.
- Alert time and duration are chosen in a dialog opened by התחל מעקב. Duration is a 10-minute slider.

## 0.7

- Material 3 layout, color, and Rubik type.
- Home and search say חיפוש קו.
- One app, version 0.7.

## 0.6

- Watch screen with a hero, a line diagram, and compact bus rows.
